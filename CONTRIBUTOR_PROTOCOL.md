# Contributor attribution

The canonical cross-project policy is maintained in [AIDB/docs/CONTRIBUTOR_ATTRIBUTION.md](https://github.com/usamaizm/AIDB/blob/main/docs/CONTRIBUTOR_ATTRIBUTION.md).

This repository's pull-request template collects the required attribution fields. Keep the authenticated GitHub actor separate from the contributor's self-declared identity. Do not infer an AI agent's identity or retroactively assign authorship without evidence.
